Proposed · Charging partner bundle

Charging, sorted from day one

One contract with Hong Seh covers the truck and its depot charger. We handle the LTA grant paperwork, the installation with our charging partner [Charge point operator], and a fleet electricity rate, all in one monthly instalment.

Why this is here: fleets named charging access as a key barrier, and Hong Seh does not yet help buyers with charger installation. A partner bundle removes that reason to wait.

  1. Site check

    We survey your depot’s power supply and parking layout.

  2. Grant handled

    We apply for the LTA EHVCG on your behalf: 50% of the cost, up to S$30,000 per charger, for heavy vehicles.

  3. Installed & commissioned

    Charger is live on the day your truck is handed over.

  4. Fleet tariff

    Charge at about S$0.35/kWh instead of S$0.78/kWh at public chargers.

Vehicle class
